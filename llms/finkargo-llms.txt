# Finkargo

> Finkargo is a Latin American foreign-trade commerce platform that helps importers overcome the operational and financial barriers to growing their import businesses. It combines supplier financing, market intelligence, supplier verification, and cargo insurance into a single import-operations workflow across Colombia and Mexico.

Note: Finkargo does not currently publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, or developer portal. This file is generated from the API Evangelist catalog profile and links to the company's public product and legal surfaces.

## Products
- [Paga — supplier financing](https://www.finkargo.com/): Alternative credit of up to USD $2.7M with 30–150 day payment terms for businesses importing over USD $70,000 annually.
- [Analiza — market intelligence](https://www.finkargo.com/): Personalized data-analytics dashboards tailored to specific industries to turn trade data into strategic decisions.
- [Verifica — supplier verification](https://www.finkargo.com/): Evaluate product quality and supplier legitimacy globally before importing.
- [Protege — cargo insurance](https://www.finkargo.com/): Warehouse-to-warehouse shipment coverage with competitive pricing and no deductibles.
